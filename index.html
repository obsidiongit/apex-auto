What We Offer

Our Detailing Services

From quick refreshes to full restorations, Apex Auto Studio delivers professional-grade mobile detailing directly to your driveway. Every service is executed with meticulous care and premium products.

Exterior Detail

Starting at $149

Hand wash, wheel and tire cleaning, trim care, and a durable spray sealant for added shine and protection.

Interior Detail

Starting at $199

Thorough vacuuming, surface cleaning, leather conditioning, light stain treatment, and interior glass cleaning.

Full Detail

Starting at $299

Our complete inside-and-out reset combining thorough interior cleaning with meticulous exterior hand wash and paint sealant.

Executive Detail

Starting at $449

The ultimate reset: clay bar decontamination, deep extraction, steam cleaning, odor elimination, and engine bay cleaning.

Paint Correction

Custom Quote

Multi-stage machine polishing to eliminate swirl marks and scratches, restoring a true mirror-like reflection to your clear coat.

Add-On Services

Starting at $50

Engine bay detail, pet hair removal, carpet extraction, steam sanitation, paint decontamination, and odor treatment.
